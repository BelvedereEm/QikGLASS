At the machine

  1. Copy the .nc file onto the controller's SD card, or open it in Candle.
  2. Screw the QWORK head into the swivel holder and clamp the holder in the spindle mount.
  3. Push the glass into the corner of the jig, smooth side up, long side left to right.
  4. Move the wheel to the jig corner and set X and Y to zero.
  5. Lower the wheel until it just pinches a sheet of paper on the glass. Set Z to zero.
  6. Put a few drops of oil in the oil cup.
  7. Wear safety glasses, start the file, and keep fingers away while it runs.
  8. Break the glass in this order: the green leftover line first (keep that strip!), then the purple relief cuts, then your blue pieces.
